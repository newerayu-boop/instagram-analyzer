Claude'ni $300/soatlik senior injenerdek ishlating

Kodingizni oddiy mashq kabi emas, production darajasida tekshiring. Mana 6 ta tayyor prompt.

Manba: @jamescoder12 (X) · o'zbekchaga Vaybecoder tayyorladi

·Qanday ishlatiladi

Har bir promptni nusxa oling va Claude'ga tashlang. Kvadrat qavs [ ] ichini o'zingiznikiga almashtiring (masalan, [language] o'rniga Python). Kodni [paste] joyiga qo'ying.

Maslahat: avval 1-promptni bering (rejimni yoqadi), keyin 2–6 ni ketma-ket ishlating.

01Rejimni yoqish

Claude'ga senior injener rolini beradi — u kodni production standartida ko'radi.

Prompt 01
Act as a staff engineer at a top tech company who has reviewed thousands of pull requests. From now on, whenever I paste code, review it with the standards of production software, not a coding exercise. My language is: [language].

02Birinchi ta'sir (60 soniya)

Senior injener 60 soniyada nima deydi — nima yaxshi, nima shubhali, nimani chiqarmaydi.

Prompt 02
Give me your first-pass reaction to my code [paste code], the way a senior would react in 60 seconds: what is fine, what smells, what is the one thing you would not let ship.

03To'g'rilik va chekka holatlar

O'rta darajali injener ko'pincha o'tkazib yuboradigan xatolarni topadi.

Prompt 03
Walk through my code [paste code] and identify the edge cases, race conditions, and failure modes a mid-level engineer is most likely to miss.

04Dizayn va chegaralar

Mas'uliyat, chegara, nomlash — nimani ajratish, birlashtirish yoki qayta nomlash kerak.

Prompt 04
Critique the design of my code [paste code] at the level of responsibilities, boundaries, naming, and what should be extracted, inlined, or renamed for future maintainers.

05Tezlik va xarajat

Haqiqatan muhim optimallashtirishlar — mayda-chuydalar emas.

Prompt 05
Profile my code [paste code] for likely hot paths, allocations, and N+1 traps, and suggest changes that meaningfully matter, not micro-optimizations.

06Namunaviy qayta yozish

Eng muhim qismni senior injener qanday yozib chiqaradi — har bir o'zgarish izohi bilan.

Prompt 06
Rewrite the highest-impact section of my code [paste section] the way a senior engineer would ship it, with a short note on why each change matters.
