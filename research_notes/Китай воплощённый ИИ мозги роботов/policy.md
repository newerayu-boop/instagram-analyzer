# Chinese state policy and support for embodied AI (具身智能) and humanoid robots, as of Nov 2026

Research date: 2026-11-09. Most sources are Chinese official or state media (Xinhua, CCTV via NDRC and stdaily, gov-linked media), plus MERICS (Apr 2026) and Bloomberg/SCMP. Where a figure comes from a media summary rather than the original document text, it is marked as such. Firecrawl was unavailable in this session, so the work used WebSearch and WebFetch only. Several original documents (the full 15th FYP outline text, the MIIT/SASAC notice, the standards guide draft) were not read in full.

## 1. National policy architecture: documents, dates, targets

### Takeaway
Embodied AI has risen in three steps. In 2023 it was a MIIT humanoid sector plan. In 2025 it entered the Government Work Report for the first time. In 2026 it became a named "future industry" in both the 15th Five-Year Plan Outline (2026–2030) and the 2026 Government Work Report. In 2026 the policy emphasis moved from "build it" to "deploy and standardize it": real-scene training, a standards system, entry/exit rules and lifecycle traceability.

### Cited Findings
**MIIT "Guiding Opinions on Innovative Development of Humanoid Robots" (人形机器人创新发展指导意见)** (older item, 2023)
- MIIT signed it on 20 Oct 2023 and published it on 2 Nov 2023. — [Xinhua 2023-11-03](https://www.xinhuanet.com/tech/20231103/e4d37192a8de419d9c2bc24b9d87dcdb/c.html); [CS.com.cn](https://cs.com.cn/xwzx/hg/202311/t20231103_6374122.html)
- **Targets for 2025:**
  - An initial innovation system is in place.
  - Breakthroughs in "brain, cerebellum, limbs" (大脑、小脑、肢体) key technologies.
  - Secure supply of core components.
  - Whole-robot products at international advanced level, with mass production.
  - Demonstration use in special-purpose, manufacturing and livelihood-service scenarios.
  - 2–3 globally influential ecosystem companies, plus a group of "specialized and sophisticated" (专精特新) SMEs.
  - 2–3 industrial clusters.
  - Source: [CS.com.cn summary](https://cs.com.cn/xwzx/hg/202311/t20231103_6374122.html)
- **Targets for 2027:**
  - Significantly stronger innovation capacity.
  - A secure and reliable supply chain.
  - An internationally competitive ecosystem and overall strength at world-advanced level.
  - Scaled development, with products "deeply integrated into the real economy".
  - Source: [CS.com.cn](https://cs.com.cn/xwzx/hg/202311/t20231103_6374122.html)
- **What "brain" means in the document:** decision-making built on AI large models. "Cerebellum" means motion control. The document also names weak spots in key base parts, the operating system, whole robots, leading firms and the ecosystem. This is a third-party summary (前瞻产业研究院). — [CS.com.cn](https://cs.com.cn/xwzx/hg/202311/t20231103_6374122.html)
- The document calls humanoids a potentially disruptive product after computers, smartphones and NEVs. — [CS.com.cn](https://cs.com.cn/xwzx/hg/202311/t20231103_6374122.html)

**Government Work Reports**
- **2025:** embodied AI appears in the Government Work Report for the first time. — [The Paper, CEAI 2026](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_32993942); [MERICS Apr 2026](https://merics.org/en/report/embodied-ai-chinas-ambitious-path-transform-its-robotics-industry)
- **2026 (March):** the report proposes "a mechanism for investment growth and risk-sharing for future industries". It then lists "培育发展未来能源、量子科技、具身智能、脑机接口、6G等未来产业" (cultivate future energy, quantum tech, embodied AI, BCI, 6G and other future industries). — [Xinhua 2026-03-08](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260308/10dd982f0a334ed7ab2e1d0bd188a93f/c.html)
- **2026 additions:** the report proposes "打造智能经济新形态" (build a new form of the intelligent economy) for the first time. It also deepens the "AI+" action. — [21st Century Business Herald](https://m.sfccn.com/2026/3-6/2MMDE1MzJfMjExMzQ2MQ.html)

**15th Five-Year Plan Outline (十五五规划纲要, adopted March 2026)**
- **Wording:** "前瞻布局未来产业. 推动量子科技、生物制造、氢能和核聚变能、脑机接口、具身智能、第六代移动通信等成为新的经济增长点." The Outline also calls for a "full-chain cultivation system" for future industries and for exploring "multiple technical routes and typical application scenarios". Source is CCTV, reprinted by NDRC on 2026-04-01. — [NDRC](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xwdt/spfg/mtjj/202604/t20260401_1404454.html)
- **Media forecasts for the plan period (not plan targets):**
  - About 100 new innovation centers and labs.
  - About 500 new 专精特新 firms.
  - Industry scale up more than 10x.
  - Annual output of "several hundred billion to trillions of yuan" by 2030.
  - More than 1 million new jobs, such as data collectors and robot-swarm operators.
  - The six future industries combined exceed 10 trillion yuan over five years.
  - These figures are CCTV's characterization, not verified against the plan text. — [NDRC/CCTV](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xwdt/spfg/mtjj/202604/t20260401_1404454.html)
- **Analyst readings of the Outline:**
  - MERICS says it names embodied AI a top "new industry track". — [MERICS](https://merics.org/en/report/embodied-ai-chinas-ambitious-path-transform-its-robotics-industry)
  - Other analyses say the "new industries and new tracks" section calls for large-scale training platforms, models, core components, and faster upgrading and deployment of humanoids. They also say humanoid commercialization is expected toward the end of the plan period. — [Design News](https://www.designnews.com/automation/china-s-15th-five-year-plan-positions-robotics-ai-to-drive-growth); [East Asia Forum 2026-04-23](https://eastasiaforum.org/2026/04/23/beijing-bets-on-embodied-intelligence-to-secure-structural-power/); [DigiChina forum](https://digichina.stanford.edu/work/forum-technology-in-chinas-15th-five-year-plan/)
  - These are secondary readings and should be checked against the original text.

**State Council "AI+" opinions (关于深入实施"人工智能+"行动的意见, 国发〔2025〕11号)** (older item, 2025)
- Issued 26 Aug 2025. It has six key actions and eight foundational support capabilities. — [10jqka](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20250827/c670668652.shtml)
- **Targets:** penetration of "new-generation intelligent terminals and agents" above 70% by 2027 and above 90% by 2030. By 2035, China "fully enters" an intelligent economy and society. — [10jqka](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20250827/c670668652.shtml)
- Intelligent robots are listed as one class of new-generation intelligent terminals, alongside ICVs, AI phones and PCs, smart home and wearables. The 70% target is **not** robot-specific. — [10jqka](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20250827/c670668652.shtml)
- MERICS describes "AI+" as treating robots as "intelligent terminals" for diffusing AI. It also notes a "Robot+" initiative and an "AI + Manufacturing" roadmap that aim at humanoid pilot lines. — [MERICS](https://merics.org/en/report/embodied-ai-chinas-ambitious-path-transform-its-robotics-industry)

**MIIT/NDRC in 2026**
- **January 2026, SCIO press conference:** MIIT Vice Minister Zhang Yunming said MIIT will speed up industry standards and an evaluation system, and will "建立健全具身智能行业准入和退出机制" (establish entry and exit mechanisms for the embodied AI industry). MIIT will also strengthen support for humanoids from the National AI Industry Investment Fund. — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103016803.html) (via search summary)
- At the same conference MIIT said there were more than 140 humanoid whole-robot makers in 2025, with more than 330 products released. — [10jqka 2026-03-03](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20260303/c675042284.shtml)
- **June 2026, MIIT and SASAC:** the "2026年度人形机器人与具身智能实景实训专项行动" notice.
  - It targets industrial, special-purpose and service scenarios.
  - Tasks: build real-scene training spaces, form innovation-application consortia, tackle job skills, and validate deployments.
  - Goals by end-2026: "百个以上高价值应用场景" (100+ high-value use cases) and "万台级规模落地能力" (capacity for deployment at the 10,000-unit scale).
  - It covers 10 key provinces/cities plus central SOEs, managed by checklist with regular evaluation.
  - Sources: [Xinhua 2026-06-12](https://www.news.cn/tech/20260612/a069b3a9948b4e0aaaa94cf3d618e545/c.html); [Gasgoo](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/news/real-world-training-two-departments-jointly-launch-special-campaign-for-humanoid-robots-and-embodied-intelligence-2064284427168104448); [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/de/news/humanoid-robots-work-mode-2026-apac-china/)
  - Its stated purpose is to optimize embodied-AI model algorithms through real scenes, accumulate high-quality real-robot data and improve components. — [Gasgoo](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/news/two-government-agencies-move-to-accelerate-humanoid-robot-development-2065328346576302081)
- An MIIT S&T Department official said MIIT will use humanoids as a "small entry point" (小切口) to drive the whole embodied-AI industry. — [Eastmoney research PDF](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202602051819786211_1.pdf) (summary only)

**Standardization**
- **December 2025:** MIIT's Humanoid Robot and Embodied AI Standardization Technical Committee (工信部人形机器人与具身智能标准化技术委员会) is established. MERICS reports five enterprise representatives were selected. — [10jqka](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20260303/c675042284.shtml); [MERICS](https://merics.org/en/report/embodied-ai-chinas-ambitious-path-transform-its-robotics-industry)
- **28 Feb 2026:** at the committee's first annual meeting it issues the 《人形机器人与具身智能标准体系（2026版）》, the first standards top-level design covering the whole chain and lifecycle.
  - Six parts: base/common; **brain-like and intelligent computing (类脑与智算)**; limbs and components; whole robot and systems; applications; safety and ethics.
  - Compiled by more than 120 institutions and companies.
  - Sources: [Xinhua 2026-03-02](https://www.news.cn/tech/20260302/3d0ff411d1d94995b4f0277d29e58e19/c.html); [stdaily 2026-03-01](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-03/01/content_478364.html)
  - Date discrepancy: one media summary dates the release to March 2026. — [10jqka](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20260303/c675042284.shtml)
- **Late May 2026:** the first national humanoid **lifecycle management service platform** launches, with the 《人形机器人全生命周期管理规范》 ("one machine, one code" traceability). — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103224444.html); [GMW 2026-06-01](https://m.gmw.cn/2026-06/01/content_38803310.htm)
- **June 2026:** the telecom-industry standard YD/T 6770—2026 "AI key base technology – embodied AI benchmark test methods" takes effect. — [GMW](https://m.gmw.cn/2026-06/01/content_38803310.htm) (via search summary)
- **24–25 Aug 2026:** MIIT releases the draft 《国家人形机器人产业标准体系建设指南（2026版）》 for comment, open 25 Aug – 23 Sep.
  - By 2028: at least 100 key standards covering capability testing and evaluation, key technologies, platforms and systems, scenario applications and safety governance.
  - More than 200 companies involved in rollout.
  - Content includes a capability-grading system, unique ID and coding, work requirements for structured and semi-structured industrial settings, and ethics and social-impact assessment.
  - Sources: [Xinhua 2026-08-24](https://www.news.cn/fortune/20260824/0e0673505fd94452ba20e18f8f97260a/c.html); [stdaily 2026-08-25](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-08/25/content_569413.html)
- **1 Nov 2026:** the data standard 《具身智能数据集质量要求及评估方法》 (embodied-AI dataset quality requirements and evaluation methods) takes effect.
  - Drafted by CAICT with more than 40 organizations.
  - Eight quality dimensions: completeness, consistency, diversity, authenticity, usability, practicality, scalability and security.
  - Source: [stdaily/CCTV 2026-08-19](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-08/19/content_565996.html)

### Inferences
- The 2026 policy mix looks like a shift from capacity-building to market discipline and deployment. It has a deployment quota (100+ scenarios, 10,000-unit scale), standards with a 2028 horizon, lifecycle IDs, and entry/exit rules. This fits a response to the late-2025 bubble warning (section 3).
- The "brain" layer is codified in the standards system as its own pillar (类脑与智算). This signals that MIIT treats models and compute as part of the humanoid industry rather than a separate AI track.

### Gaps
- The full text of the 15th FYP Outline section on embodied AI was not read. I cannot confirm any quantitative humanoid target in the plan itself, and none was found.
- The texts of the MIIT/SASAC June 2026 notice and the August 2026 draft guide were read only through Xinhua and stdaily summaries.
- The final (non-draft) version of the national standards guide was not checked. It may have been finalized after September 2026.
- The concrete content of the "entry and exit mechanism" was not found. As of the sources it is still a stated direction, with no published rules.

## 2. State money: funds, subsidies, procurement

### Takeaway
State capital reaches the sector mainly in three ways: national funds (the National VC Guidance Fund and the National AI Industry Investment Fund), local government guidance funds (Beijing, Hubei and others), and SOE and state-investor participation in startup rounds. Procurement and subsidies for buyers are local and fragmented. Private and state money combined is very large: about CNY 93.5bn in H1 2026 by one count.

### Cited Findings
- **National Venture Capital Guidance Fund, plus three Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei regional funds:** about CNY 1 trillion (about EUR 120bn) over 20 years, according to MERICS. — [MERICS](https://merics.org/en/report/embodied-ai-chinas-ambitious-path-transform-its-robotics-industry)
  - No report was found of this fund itself investing in a specific humanoid or embodied-AI company.
- **National AI Industry Investment Fund (国家人工智能产业投资基金, often called the "Big Fund III" AI vehicle):**
  - In early March 2026 it made its **first embodied-AI investment**, joining Galbot's (银河通用) **CNY 2.5bn** round.
  - Other state-linked co-investors: Sinopec, CITIC Investment Holdings, BOC Asset Investment, SAIC Financial Holdings, SMIC Capital (中芯聚源), Yizhuang State-owned Investment, Kunpeng Fund, Wuxi VC, Fujian Industrial Investment and Chengdu S&T VC.
  - Galbot reports customers including CATL, Bosch, Toyota, BAIC and SAIC, with cumulative orders of "thousands of units".
  - Source: [10jqka 2026-03-03](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20260303/c675042284.shtml)
  - The fund's size was not stated in the sources found.
- In January 2026 MIIT pledged to strengthen the National AI Industry Investment Fund's support for humanoids. — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103016803.html)
- **Beijing:** a CNY 100bn government investment fund that prioritizes AI and robotics. This is an April 2025 report (older item). — [PEdaily 2025-04](https://news.pedaily.cn/202504/548303.shtml) (via search summary)
- **Beijing E-Town:** subsidies for robot buyers (SCMP, Aug 2025, cited by MERICS). — [MERICS](https://merics.org/en/report/embodied-ai-chinas-ambitious-path-transform-its-robotics-industry)
- **Hubei:** the Hubei Humanoid Robot Industry Investment Fund subscribed CNY 300m (15%) of a CNY 2bn embodied-robot private fund set up in Shanghai on 20 Mar 2026. — [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3878012843307009) / [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4872074.html) (via search summary)
- **Subsidies:** some provinces and cities subsidize up to 30% of automation-innovation project costs or offer humanoid purchase discounts. — [MERICS](https://merics.org/en/report/embodied-ai-chinas-ambitious-path-transform-its-robotics-industry)
- **Capital flow totals:**
  - 2025: 760 embodied-robot financing events (CCID Consulting). — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103016803.html) (via search summary)
  - The first two months of 2026 equaled half of all 2025 embodied-robot investment (CCTV). — [NDRC/CCTV](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xwdt/spfg/mtjj/202604/t20260401_1404454.html)
  - H1 2026: about CNY 93.5bn across 322 deals (Huxiu count). Beijing had 100 deals and CNY 35.5bn, Shenzhen 61 deals and CNY 23.8bn, Hangzhou 34 deals and CNY 9.2bn. Beijing, Guangdong and Shanghai together took 79.17% of the money. — [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4872074.html)
  - Another count gives more than CNY 67.7bn by early June 2026. — [Xinhua 2026-06-12](https://www.news.cn/tech/20260612/a069b3a9948b4e0aaaa94cf3d618e545/c.html) (via search summary)
  - These counts use different methods, so the figures differ.

### Inferences
- The National AI Industry Investment Fund's entry into Galbot, together with SMIC Capital and auto and energy SOEs, suggests state capital favors firms with industrial customers and supply-chain ties. Pure demo startups appear less favored, which fits the anti-bubble line.
- The 2026 Government Work Report's "investment growth and risk-sharing mechanism for future industries" points to more state co-investment and risk-absorbing instruments. The specific mechanism was not found.

### Gaps
- No verified sizes were found for dedicated Shanghai, Shenzhen or Hangzhou embodied-AI funds, or for Hubei's humanoid fund as a whole.
- The National VC Guidance Fund's launch date and its robotics allocations were not verified in this session. MERICS gives only the headline amount.
- No consolidated data on government procurement of humanoids (as opposed to SOE deployment under the MIIT/SASAC action) was found.

## 3. NDRC bubble warning (Nov 2025) and 2026 responses

### Takeaway
On 27 Nov 2025 the NDRC publicly warned of bubble and duplication risk among about 150 humanoid makers. In 2026 the policy response has been standards, entry/exit rules (announced but not detailed), lifecycle traceability, and directing funds and deployment toward real scenarios. No explicit cap on company numbers or forced consolidation order was found.

### Cited Findings
- **The warning:** NDRC spokesperson Li Chao, at a 27 Nov 2025 briefing, said more than 150 companies are building humanoids, and the number is rising. He said the country must prevent "highly similar" products from flooding the market and must keep R&D from being crowded out. — [Bloomberg 2025-11-27](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-27/china-warns-of-bubble-risk-in-booming-humanoid-robotics-industry); [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/economy/china-economy/article/3334440/chinas-top-economic-planning-agency-warns-rise-too-many-robots)
- MERICS describes the same briefing as warning of overcapacity and reduced R&D funding, and calling to prevent "piled-up" listings of repetitive products. — [MERICS](https://merics.org/en/report/embodied-ai-chinas-ambitious-path-transform-its-robotics-industry)
- **Stated response at the time:** strengthen policy guidance toward "healthy and standardised development". Reported measures include market entry and exit, core-tech R&D, testing infrastructure, and consolidation and resource sharing. The last list comes from an aggregator and was not verified against the transcript. — [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/china-warns-humanoid-robot-firms/); [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/china-warns-of-bubble-risk-as-150-companies-flood-humanoid-robot-market/)
- **Company counts:** MIIT's count in January 2026 was "more than 140" whole-robot makers in 2025. The NDRC said 150+. The difference is likely one of definition. — [10jqka](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20260303/c675042284.shtml)
- **2026 follow-ups (see section 1 for details):**
  - Entry and exit mechanism announced (Jan 2026).
  - Standards system (Feb 2026).
  - Lifecycle management code (May 2026).
  - Deployment-oriented real-scene training action (Jun 2026).
  - National standards guide draft with a 2028 horizon (Aug 2026).
  - Sources: [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103016803.html); [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/fortune/20260824/0e0673505fd94452ba20e18f8f97260a/c.html)
- Funding grew rather than cooled after the warning: the first two months of 2026 equaled half of 2025's total. — [NDRC/CCTV](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xwdt/spfg/mtjj/202604/t20260401_1404454.html)

### Inferences
- The warning did not slow capital. The state's answer is to channel the boom through standards, real-use quotas and traceability rather than to restrict entry outright. Watch whether the entry/exit rules turn into a "whitelist" or qualification catalog, as with past Chinese industries such as NEVs.

### Gaps
- No published entry/exit criteria were found. No explicit "防止重复建设" (anti-duplicate-construction) document specific to humanoids was found.
- The original NDRC transcript was not read.

## 4. Local programs and training grounds (data-collection "training fields")

### Takeaway
Infrastructure is spreading fast. CAICT (Aug 2026) counts more than 70 embodied-AI training grounds in operation and more than 40 under construction or planned, covering over half of China's provinces. They cluster in the Yangtze River Delta, Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei and the Pearl River Delta. Beijing and Shanghai anchor the national centers, Tiangong and Qinglong/OpenLoong, which open-source hardware, models and data. Real-world data is still small relative to need: about 0.5m hours of compliant real-interaction data, against "tens of millions" of hours needed.

### Cited Findings
**Training grounds (national)**
- CAICT's 《具身智能训练场研究报告（2026年）》 (18 Aug 2026) reports:
  - More than 70 training grounds built and in operation; more than 40 under construction or planned.
  - Three core clusters: YRD, BTH and PRD.
  - Coverage of more than half of provincial-level regions, spreading into third-tier and lower cities.
  - A pipeline running from collection through annotation, processing, training and evaluation to real-robot deployment.
  - Source: [stdaily/CCTV 2026-08-19](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-08/19/content_565996.html); [The Paper](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33815394)
- Yicai reports that Zhejiang has the most training grounds. — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103337172.html) (headline via search; not read)
- **Data volume:**
  - Guizhou's Big Data Bureau (July 2026) put China's compliant real physical-interaction data at only about 500,000 hours. It said commercial deployment needs tens of millions of hours, a gap above 99%. — [The Paper / Sina](https://finance.sina.cn/stock/jdts/2026-10-09/detail-iniuqqhr7112595.d.html) (via search summary)
  - A Zhongguancun (Beijing) firm's training ground has delivered 1.5 million hours of human video data. High-quality model training may need tens to hundreds of millions of hours. — [stdaily](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-08/19/content_565996.html)
  - Lingqi/"黎曼动力" (as reported), with Lightwheel (光轮智能) and Noitom Robotics, plans to collect and train on 1 million hours by end-2026. — [10jqka 2026-10-19](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20261019/c680808700.shtml) (via search summary)
  - 《具身数据发展报告2026》 counts more than 100 embodied-data startups. — same source, via search summary
- **Xiong'an:** a training ground opened in December 2025, where data collectors repeat each action "over a thousand times". — [The Paper](https://m.thepaper.cn/detail/33737007) (via search summary)
- MERICS notes robotics training centers that coordinate training data for startups in Beijing, Shanghai and Hefei, plus AgiBot's own data-generation facilities, including its Shanghai center. — [MERICS](https://merics.org/en/report/embodied-ai-chinas-ambitious-path-transform-its-robotics-industry)

**Beijing**
- **Beijing Humanoid Robot Innovation Center (北京人形):**
  - Released **Tiangong 3.0 (具身天工3.0)** on 10 Feb 2026: 169 cm, 62 kg, 43 DoF. It runs on the in-house general embodied platform **Huisi Kaiwu (慧思开物)**. — [NCSTI 2026-02-12](https://www.ncsti.gov.cn/kjdt/scyq/bjjjjskfq/jkdt/202602/t20260212_238199.html); [BJNews](https://m.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1770704492129849.html)
  - It has **open-sourced** the Tiangong body, motion-control framework, **world model**, large models and training toolchain, and datasets. — [Xinhua 2026-07-24](https://www.news.cn/tech/20260724/dde0463fd1ea4742af9f4a45cf5ff7c9/c.html) (via search summary)
  - In June 2026, with D-Robotics (地瓜机器人), it announced Tiangong 3.0 mass delivery in H2 2026 on the **domestic Xuri (旭日) S600 chip**. — [ITHome](https://www.ithome.com/0/961/640.htm) (via search summary)
- The Tiangong platform won the first human–humanoid half-marathon. This was Beijing E-Town, April 2025, older item. — [Xinhua 2026-09-10](https://www.news.cn/20260910/8be430b664db469594ce1c354e63484b/c.html) (via search summary)
- The Beijing Humanoid Robot Data Training Center was featured during the 2026 Two Sessions (a Kuafu robot folding clothes). — [Xinhua 2026-03-08](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260308/10dd982f0a334ed7ab2e1d0bd188a93f/c.html)
- In H1 2026 Beijing was the top city for embodied-AI financing (100 deals, CNY 35.5bn). — [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4872074.html)

**Shanghai**
- **National-Local Co-built Humanoid Robot Innovation Center (国地中心):**

## 5. What is explicitly prioritized for the "brain" (models, world models, data, simulation, compute/chips, OS)

### Takeaway
Explicit "brain" priorities in official documents are:
- "Brain/cerebellum" breakthroughs (2023 MIIT opinions).
- Large-scale training platforms and models (15th FYP reading).
- A dedicated "brain-like and intelligent computing" pillar in the 2026 standards system.
- A data-quality standard.
- Real-scene data accumulation (MIIT/SASAC 2026).

Open-sourcing by the state-backed centers (Beijing: world model, large models, datasets; Shanghai: OpenLoong) is the practical channel. Domestic chips appear in deployments (Tiangong 3.0 on the Xuri S600), but no national document found here sets a specific domestic-chip quota for robots.

### Cited Findings
- **2023 MIIT opinions:** the "brain" is decision-making based on AI large models and the "cerebellum" is motion control. The operating system is named as a weak spot. — [CS.com.cn](https://cs.com.cn/xwzx/hg/202311/t20231103_6374122.html)
- **2026 standards system:** a dedicated "类脑与智算" (brain-like and intelligent computing) part, one of six. — [Xinhua 2026-03-02](https://www.news.cn/tech/20260302/3d0ff411d1d94995b4f0277d29e58e19/c.html)
- **15th FYP:** large-scale training platforms and AI models are listed under new industries and tracks (secondary reading). The Outline also calls for exploring multiple technical routes. — [Design News](https://www.designnews.com/automation/china-s-15th-five-year-plan-positions-robotics-ai-to-drive-growth); [NDRC/CCTV](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xwdt/spfg/mtjj/202604/t20260401_1404454.html)
- **MIIT/SASAC 2026 action:** optimize embodied-AI model algorithms through real-scene training and accumulate high-quality real-robot data. — [Gasgoo](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/news/two-government-agencies-move-to-accelerate-humanoid-robot-development-2065328346576302081)

### Inferences
- The state's "brain" strategy is mainly about infrastructure and the commons:
  - Data training grounds and data standards.
  - Open-source models and datasets from national and local co-built centers.
  - Standards for "brain-like and intelligent computing".
  - Deployment quotas that generate real data.
- It is not about picking a single national foundation model.
- Compute and chip localization for on-robot inference is a visible gap, given Nvidia Jetson Thor dependence. Domestic alternatives (D-Robotics S600 and others) are entering through state-center products. Expect policy attention here, given 15th FYP self-reliance themes, but no explicit robot-chip target was found.

### Gaps
- No MIIT document was found that specifically mandates world-model or VLA "brain" projects with funding amounts, such as a dedicated 具身大模型 special program.
- No policy text was found on a national robot operating system, beyond the 2023 note naming the OS as a weak spot.
- No explicit domestic-chip localization targets for robots were found.
- Not checked: whether MOST national key R&D programs for 2026 contain embodied-AI "brain" projects and their budgets.
